I am a PhD candidate in the [HCI Initiative](https://hci.cse.ust.hk/index.html) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. [Xiaojuan Ma](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~mxj/). I began my PhD studies in 2024.

Prior to joining HKUST, I earned my master’s degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2024, conducting research in [DALAB](http://dalab.se.sjtu.edu.cn/www/home/) under the supervision of Prof. [Xubo Yang](http://dalab.se.sjtu.edu.cn/www/home/?page_id=143). I also earned my bachelor’s degree from SJTU in 2021.

My research interests lie in Extended Reality (XR) interaction, including VR, AR, and MR. My work has explored human perception and interaction in immersive environments, including spatial perception, hand redirection, and passive haptics.
